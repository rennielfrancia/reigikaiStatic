Karate Classes in Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas & Clark County

Reigikai SKIF - Premier Shotokan Karate Training

Looking for the best karate classes in the Las Vegas valley? Reigikai SKIF offers traditional Shotokan karate training for students from Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Paradise, Boulder City, Enterprise, Whitney, Sunrise Manor, and throughout Clark County, Nevada.

Why Choose Reigikai SKIF for Karate Classes?

✅ Authentic Traditional Training: Official SKIF (Shotokan Karate-Do International Federation) branch school
✅ Experienced Instructors: Certified black belt instructors with decades of experience
✅ All Ages Welcome: Programs for kids, teens, and adults
✅ Convenient Location: Chuck Minker Sports Complex - easily accessible from all Clark County areas
✅ Proven Results: Our students compete successfully in national and international tournaments
✅ Family-Friendly: Safe, supportive environment for the whole family

Areas We Serve in Clark County:
• Las Vegas: Downtown, The Strip, Westside, Eastside
• Henderson: Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, MacDonald Ranch
• North Las Vegas: Aliante, Eldorado, Shadow Creek
• Summerlin: The Ridges, Red Rock, Trails Village, Willows
• Spring Valley: Spanish Trail, Desert Shores, Peccole Ranch
• Paradise: University District, Eastside, Winchester
• Boulder City: Historic District, Lake Las Vegas area
• Enterprise: Southwest Las Vegas valley
• Whitney: East Las Vegas valley
• Sunrise Manor: Northeast Las Vegas valley

What You'll Learn in Our Karate Classes:
• Traditional Shotokan karate techniques and forms (kata)
• Self-defense skills and situational awareness
• Physical fitness, flexibility, and coordination
• Mental discipline and focus
• Respect, courtesy, and traditional values
• Competition preparation (optional)
• Belt progression from white belt to black belt

• Saturdays: 10:30 AM - 12:30 PM
