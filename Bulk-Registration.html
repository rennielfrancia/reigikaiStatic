We're also offering a bulk registration option for clubs and teams to simplify the process. If you're registering multiple athletes, please follow the instructions below:

✅ Bulk Registration Instructions

1

📧 Send Email

Please email reigikai@gmail.com

Subject line: "Bulk Registration – [Your Club Name]"

2

📋 Include Athlete Details

Include the following details for each athlete:

  • Full Name
  • Gender
  • Age or Birthday
  • Ranking (choose one):
    • Beginner (10th kyu – 7th kyu)
    • Novice (6th kyu – 4th kyu)
    • Intermediate (3rd kyu – 1st kyu)
    • Advanced/Elite (Shodan and up)
  • Event: Kata, Kumite, or Both
3

📄 Waiver Requirement

All athletes must submit a signed waiver form and bring it on the day of the tournament. The waiver is attached to this email.

4

💰 Payment & Confirmation

Once we receive your list, we'll reply with your total fee, payment instructions, and confirmation summary for your team.
